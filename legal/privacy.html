Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 | NextGen School Alert (A2Z Online Services Pvt Ltd)

Welcome to NextGen School Alert (accessible via schoolalarm.online). We deeply value your trust and are committed to safeguarding the personal data of school administrators, educators, parents, and students.

1. Information We Collect

To provide seamless multi-school management and automated alerts, we collect institutional data, personal identifiers, and technical telemetry via Cloudflare and Supabase.

2. How We Use Your Information

We utilize the gathered data strictly for legitimate operational purposes, setting up ERP portals, and delivering automated attendance or fee notifications.

3. Data Storage & Infrastructure Security

All data processed through NextGen School Alert is securely hosted on high-performance cloud databases powered by Supabase and secured by Cloudflare network shields.

4. Children’s Data Protection

Our platform handles student records as instructed by educational institutions. We do not engage in targeted advertising, behavioral profiling, or commercial exploitation of children's data.
