3. Our Dual Role Under the DPDP Act

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, data governance is categorized between the entity that decides the purpose of data processing (Data Fiduciary) and the entity handling technical execution (Data Processor):

Data Classification Our Role Governing Authority
Student, parent, and teacher records entered into the ERP (e.g., bio-data, admissions, attendance, marks, fees, bus tracking, notices). Data Processor The School (Data Fiduciary). We act solely under documented institutional instructions.
Public website inquiries, product demo bookings, partner onboarding, administrative billing, and technical security logs. Data Fiduciary NextGen School Alert (A2Z Online Services Pvt Ltd) under this direct policy.

4. Information We Collect

A. Information You Submit

When booking a demonstration, requesting trial access, or registering an institution, we collect:

  • Full name, official designation, contact telephone/mobile number, and email address.
  • School name, administrative branch details, city, state, affiliated education board, and student strength.
  • Specific operational modules required (e.g., fee collection, auto-bell system, GPS fleet management).

B. Automatically Collected Technical Data

When interacting with our cloud infrastructure, our servers and network shields (via Cloudflare and Supabase) automatically record telemetry necessary for performance and intrusion detection. This includes IP addresses, browser types, operating systems, session timestamps, and referral headers.

C. Mobile Application Logs

When staff or parents access portal apps, diagnostic data (such as app version, device architecture, crash traces, and push notification tokens) is captured solely to verify session authenticity and dispatch alerts.

5. Cookies and Web Storage

7. How We Handle School Data

The school retains uncompromised ownership of all student, parent, and institutional data. We pledge the following commitments within every institutional Service Agreement:

01. Institutional Instructions Only

We process school databases strictly on written directions from verified school management.

02. No AI Training

School records are never used to train, fine-tune, or validate third-party AI models.

03. Absolute Data Segregation

Multi-tenant architectures ensure complete database isolation; no school can access another campus's data.

04. Never Sold or Monetized

We never sell, lease, or license records to third-party commercial vendors or marketers.

05. Zero Advertising SDKs

No ad networks, behavioral trackers, or marketing analytics exist within our software.

06. Indian Infrastructure

All active production databases, secondary backups, and access logs reside in secure Indian data centers.

07. Masked Contact Details

Parent and teacher personal phone numbers are masked by default to ensure communication privacy.

08. Complete Data Portability & Purge

Upon contract expiration, institutions receive complete structured data exports followed by certified system purges.

8. Children's Data Protection

Under India's DPDP Act, any individual under the age of 18 is classified as a child. Given that student management is a cornerstone of our software:

  • The educational institution is responsible for securing verifiable parental consent prior to digital enrollment.
  • We enforce a total prohibition on behavioral tracking, targeted commercial messaging, and minor profiling.
  • System access to sensitive student medical, academic, or disciplinary notes is restricted strictly to designated teachers and administrators via role-based credentials.

9. Service Providers & Sub-processors

To deliver a scalable, enterprise-grade cloud experience, we work with enterprise-grade infrastructure providers bound by contractual non-disclosure and security mandates:

  • Cloud Database & Storage: Supabase Cloud Infrastructure (Enterprise PostgreSql with Row Level Security).

10. Where Your Data is Stored

All primary school databases, secondary failovers, and operational logs remain within enterprise-grade data facilities physically located in the India region. We do not store, duplicate, or maintain student records outside Indian territory.

11. Data Retention & Archival

  • General Inquiries: Contact information provided during demo requests is archived for reference and deleted upon request.
  • School Records: Managed throughout the duration of the institutional contract. When a school offboards, data exports are provided, and production databases are purged within statutory timelines.
  • Financial & Audit Logs: Access logs, transaction receipts, and fiscal audit records are retained as mandated by applicable regulatory guidelines.

12. Security Safeguards

NextGen School Alert deploys layered defense mechanisms to prevent data interception, compromise, or corruption:

  • End-to-end transport encryption utilizing modern TLS 1.3 cryptographic suites across all endpoints.
  • Row Level Security (RLS) enforcement on all active database tables, ensuring users access only their assigned institutional partitions.
  • Role-based access permissions restricting sensitive actions (such as student exit gate passes) behind Multi-Factor Authentication (OTP verification).

13. Security Incident Protocol

In the unlikely event of a confirmed security incident affecting institutional records, NextGen School Alert maintains a rapid-response protocol: notifying affected institutions within 24 hours of confirmation, furnishing technical details to support regulatory notifications to the Data Protection Board of India, and taking immediate remediation measures.

14. Your Statutory Rights

Under the DPDP Act, eligible data principals retain statutory rights regarding their personal information:

  • Right to Access: Review summaries of personal data maintained within system logs.
  • Right to Correction & Updating: Rectify inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete records.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of personal records when the processing purpose is fulfilled, subject to administrative and educational statutory retention rules.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Submit formal grievances to our assigned protection officers.

Note for Parents and Students: Because student records are controlled by your educational institution, correction or deletion requests should be directed to your school administration. We execute such requests upon institutional instruction.
