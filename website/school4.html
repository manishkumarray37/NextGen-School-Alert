Kindergarten

Ages 5–6

Kindergarten is about falling in love with school. Children learn through play, stories, and hands-on projects while building letter sounds, number sense, and friendships.

  • Daily phonics in small groups of four to six
  • Counting, sorting and shapes with hands-on materials
  • Choice time in block, art and reading corners
  • Weekly afternoon in the outdoor garden classroom

The Kindergarten Café: children plan a menu, make price tags and run a pretend café for their families.
